FAQ

Before we talk

I don't have a tech team. What happens after the POC?

You get the code, the documentation and a clear explanation of how to move forward. I can help define the profile of your first developer or assess a hired team for the next phase — so you don't depend on me.

Do you help with funding applications?

Yes. In the proof of concept and prototype packages, I can prepare the technical part of the proposal: context, feasibility backed by the POC evidence, technology readiness and timeline. See a condensed example of a technical document for a funding call.

Who owns the code and the intellectual property?

You do. The code developed for the project is delivered to you, and this is set out in the contract. Generic tools I already had before the project may be used as a foundation, without limiting your use.

How much does the first conversation cost?

The initial technical conversation has no cost and no commitment — it is just a conversation, with no development or deliverables. If it makes sense to move forward, the package scope and price are agreed afterwards, in writing.

Will my idea stay confidential?

Yes. I am happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA), even before you go into detail.

What is the difference between a POC, a prototype and an MVP?

A POC proves that the core mechanism works. A prototype shows how the solution would be used and is meant for presenting. An MVP is a minimal product running with real customers. I can take you through all three stages; after the MVP, ongoing development and operation are handled by your team or a hired team.

Why part-time?

I am a professor at UFLA under an exclusive-dedication contract. Brazil's Federal Teaching Career Law (Law 12,772/2012, art. 21) allows professors under this regime to engage in occasional collaboration with companies, in their field of expertise, for up to 8 hours per week and 416 hours per year. This was reinforced by Brazil's Legal Framework for Science, Technology and Innovation (Law 13,243/2016), created to bring universities and industry closer together.

That is why I work with a fixed scope and few projects at once — which also keeps the focus on each one.

Each project is authorized in advance by the university, under UFLA's Resolution CUNI 67/2021, so work starts a few weeks after we agree on the scope.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. All the work can be done remotely, for clients anywhere in Brazil or abroad, with online meetings for the demos.

What if the POC shows the idea doesn't work?

That is a result too — and usually the cheapest one you will ever get. The report explains why and, where they exist, the alternative paths.
